The confirmation contract

You pressed submit. Here is what happens.

Every registration follows the same three steps, whichever activity it is for. This page is the contract: what arrives, when, and exactly what to do if it does not.

1. A confirmation email arrives

Your registration lands the moment you submit it, and a confirmation email follows to the address you gave, usually within minutes. It comes from the system you registered on, so check the inbox of the email address you typed into the form.

2. If a place is limited, the email says so

Some activities cap their numbers. If a group is full, your confirmation says so plainly, and you keep your place in line. If a spot opens, we email you. You never need to re-register.

3. If nothing arrives within one school day

  • First, check your spam or junk folder. Confirmation emails sometimes land there.
  • Search your inbox for your child's name. Some systems put it in the subject line.
  • Still nothing? Email with your child's name and the activity. We will confirm your registration by hand.

An unanswered registration is our problem to fix, never a reason to submit the form again. One registration per child per activity keeps every list clean.

Worried a payment message is not from us?

Registration and payment messages only come from official ELC channels. If anything feels off, read how to know it is really us before you pay anything.
