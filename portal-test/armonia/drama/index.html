Armonia · Drama

Every child on stage.

Drama at Armonia is part of one package: music, drama and dance performance, together. Children work with voice, character and movement, and along the way they grow in confidence and learn to carry a piece as a team.

What a session feels like
A rehearsal room, not a classroom.
Sessions run in small groups, and the same group returns week after week, so a scene has time to grow. Children try a moment one way and then another: how a line sounds, how a character stands, how a movement lands. Getting it wrong in front of friends is part of the work, and that is where the confidence comes from.
What a performance feels like

The performances children work toward are ELC occasions, with the whole school behind them. At a drama night nobody auditions for a place: every child takes a part, and every child gets a moment at the centre of the stage. A Night in Sherwood filled the hall with families for an evening the children carried from start to finish. Read the story on our blog.

In the Theatre of the Imagination, ELC's drama and performance atelier, children experiment with light, shadow and photography as ways of exploring their own ideas. Inside Armonia, performances anchor the year.
