The Portal · project window

What we are building.

The ELC Portal is being built as the one place for families for everything beyond the classroom. A live test build is running now, one level up from this page. We are building it in the open, and this window shows exactly what is moving. Leadership and staff can vote on what comes next from the idea board. In August 2026 the Portal goes live for all families.
