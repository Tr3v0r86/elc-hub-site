Privacy

What this site knows about you: nothing.

No login. No accounts. No cookies. No trackers. No child data. This page explains what that means, and where your information does live when you sign up for something.

A promise you can check

This site is a set of plain pages. It sets no cookies, keeps no accounts, loads no advertising scripts, and serves everything, including its fonts, from its own address. We count which pages get opened so we know what helps; the counter stores the page address and nothing else; there is no cookie, no fingerprint, and nothing that identifies you, and you can verify this in your browser's developer tools.

If you install this site as an app, the pages are stored on your own device so they open offline. Only that page-open count is ever sent back to us.

Where your information does go

Some links on this site take you to the systems that run registration and feedback. Each one only ever holds what you type into it:

  • Registration forms (ASA, activities and events): the details you enter are handled under the school's data privacy policy.
  • The feedback button: sends only the message you write. Add your name if you want a reply; nothing is attached automatically.
  • Add to calendar: the calendar file is made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
