Hopes and Wishes

Meet your child's teacher.

The year begins with a conversation. Hopes and Wishes is a twenty minute meeting with your child's teacher: what you want this year to hold for your child, in your words. No reports, no slides, just you and the person who will spend the year beside them. Find your class below and book your time.
