This week  /  Quick answers
Who do I ask

Every question has one right door.

Find your situation, and you have the answer and the person in one line. If it is not here, tell us with the feedback button and we will add it.

The school day
My child will be absent or late today
Email the school office in the morning with your child's name and class. A short line is enough.
I am running late for pickup
Call your campus office so your child knows the plan.
Something my child lost or left behind
Email the office with a short description. Found items wait at reception.
Is the school open as normal today
The status page always has the answer, and a banner appears on every page here when anything changes.
Activities and sign-ups
A question about ASA, Armonia or sport
The Activities page has every programme and its sign-up. For anything it does not answer:
I registered and heard nothing back
Read what happens after you register: what arrives, when, and what to do if it does not.
When do sign-ups open
The dated strip on the This week page counts down to every open date. Sport sign-up opens 7 September.
Money and messages
A bill, a payment request, or an invoice question
Email the office directly, using an address you already know. First read how to know a message is really from us.
My contact details changed
Email the office with the new details and your child's class.
A message asking for money looks odd
Stop, and check how to know it is really us. When in doubt, pay nothing and call the office.
Something serious
I am worried about a child's safety or wellbeing
Go straight to the report a concern page. It explains who to speak to and what happens next.
Something on this site is wrong or missing
Use the feedback button in the corner of any page. It comes straight to the team that builds this site.

Campus phone numbers are being added to this page. Until then, every campus number is on the school noticeboard and in the parent handbook.
