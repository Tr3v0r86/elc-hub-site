Who collects your child

A card for the person at the gate.

Sometimes a nanny, a driver or a grandparent collects your child. Print this pocket card for them. It says who they are collecting, in English and Thai, and it gives the office contact if anything needs checking. You write your child's name and class on it by hand: the card itself holds no personal details.

It prints pocket sized (A6). Print one for each person who collects. The card helps at the gate: it does not replace telling the school office who is coming.
