Try-outs and squads, in plain words.

What a try-out is, what it is not, and the season ahead: the dates, the four sports, and where sign-up happens.

What a try-out is
TRY
A try-out is a session
Your child trains with the coaches, and the coaches see their level and where they fit. That is all it is: a normal session with people watching carefully.
TRY
A try-out is not a place
Squads are selected, so trying out does not guarantee a spot. Every child gets clear feedback either way, so you know where things stand and what comes next.
